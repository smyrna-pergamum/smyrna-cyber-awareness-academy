Smyrna Cyber Academy is a static, browser-based learning app. It teaches practical cybersecurity habits through short lessons, separate quizzes and realistic case studies. No account or server is required. Learning progress is saved in your browser's local storage.

The interface and all 28 trainings are available in **Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Arabic and Russian**. Case study explanations teach safe decisions without revealing quiz answers.

## Quick start

1. **Online:** open the [live academy](https://smyrna-pergamum.github.io/smyrna-cyber-awareness-academy/).
2. **Offline:** [download the source ZIP](https://github.com/smyrna-pergamum/smyrna-cyber-awareness-academy/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), extract it and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Choose a language, create a profile or continue as a guest, then open a training.
4. Read the lesson and case study, complete its quiz, and follow your progress in the app.

The core app works offline because its HTML, JavaScript, translations and styles are included in the repository. When online, it loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts; without a connection, it falls back to system fonts.

## Features

- **28 practical trainings:** phishing, passwords, multi-factor authentication, social engineering, data protection, devices, remote work and more.
- **Teaching lessons:** clear objectives and modular explanations that teach the topic without giving away quiz answers.
- **Case studies:** a realistic workplace story for every training, followed by a safe-response discussion.
- **Separate quizzes:** three questions per training with answer feedback.
- **Progress and achievements:** profiles, XP, badges, streaks and quiz scores saved in the browser.
- **Completion certificate:** available after all trainings are completed and every quiz is passed with a perfect score.
- **Seven languages:** Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Arabic and Russian.
- **Accessible themes and layout:** responsive screens, light and dark themes, and right-to-left Arabic support.
- **Offline use:** open the downloaded `index.html` without a web server or build step.

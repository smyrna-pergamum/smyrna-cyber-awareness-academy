Smyrna Cyber Academy is a static, browser-based learning app. It teaches practical cybersecurity habits through short lessons, separate quizzes and realistic case studies. No account or server is required. Learning progress is saved in your browser's local storage.

## Quick start

1. **Online:** open the [live academy](https://smyrna-pergamum.github.io/smyrna-cyber-awareness-academy/).
2. **Offline:** [download the source ZIP](https://github.com/smyrna-pergamum/smyrna-cyber-awareness-academy/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), extract it and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Choose a language, create a profile or continue as a guest, then open a training.
4. Read the lesson and case study, complete its quiz, and follow your progress in the app.

The core app works offline. When online, it loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts; without a connection, it falls back to system fonts.

## Features

- 28 practical trainings, each with objectives, teaching explanations, a case study and a separate quiz.
- Progress tracking, XP, badges and scores saved in the browser.
- Certificate after all trainings and perfect quiz results are completed.
- Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Arabic and Russian.
- Responsive layout, light and dark themes, and right-to-left Arabic support.
